the view a few minutes ago
What this is. A 2012 iMac running Linux sits at a first-floor window in a Newcastle arcade, pointed at a glass dome, two brick walls, two satellite dishes and a wedge of sky. It watches a few frames a second. When something small moves against that fixed scene it keeps a boxed crop and a timestamp, and that is the register. The view itself is published every few minutes. Nothing else is stored, and raw frames never leave the office.

Bird names are drawn from the authors and characters on the library shelves in the same room. Species are impressions from size and tone, nothing more. A bird is "likely" the same as an earlier one when it uses the same perch at the same size and tone within three weeks, which is as close to recognition as this camera gets.
