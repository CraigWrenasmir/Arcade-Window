the view a few minutes ago
What this is. An old iMac sits at the window of Craig's office in a Newcastle arcade. It looks out at a glass roof, two brick walls, two satellite dishes and a patch of sky. A few times a second it checks whether anything has moved. When a bird does, it saves a small picture with a box around it, and the time.

Every bird is named after a character in a book on Craig's shelf. The species is a rough guess from size and colour. If a bird of the same size and colour lands on the same spot within three weeks, the register assumes it is the same bird. It is often wrong.
