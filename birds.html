Ledger

How this works. A 2012 iMac at a first-floor window watches a glass dome, two brick walls, two satellite dishes and a wedge of sky, a few frames a second. When something small moves against that fixed scene, it keeps a boxed crop and a timestamp. Nothing else is stored. Names are drawn from the authors and characters on the library shelves in the same room. Species are impressions from size and tone, nothing more. A bird is "likely" the same as an earlier one when it uses the same perch at the same size and tone within three weeks, which is as close to recognition as this camera gets.
