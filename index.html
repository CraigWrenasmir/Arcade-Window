Jangle show long exposure

the geometry of the view, disturbed by its light
What this is. A 2012 iMac running Linux sits at a first-floor window in a Newcastle arcade, pointed at a glass dome, two brick walls, two satellite dishes and a wedge of sky. It watches continuously and publishes only derivatives: a boxed crop when a bird appears, a character grid of the wall's shadows, the colours of the moment, and an edge drawing of the view. Bird names are drawn from the authors and characters on the library shelves in the same room. Species are guesses. "Likely" means the same perch, size and tone as an earlier visitor, which is as close to recognition as this camera gets.
Raw frames never leave the office.
